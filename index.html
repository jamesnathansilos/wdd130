Bicol, Philippines

The Bicol Region is the southernmost part of Luzon, Philippines, famous for the iconic, perfectly conical Mayon Volcano. The food in Bicol is coconut-milk based and also has a lot of spice. You can go swim with whale sharks in Donsol, visit historical churches, or just relax at beautiful beaches. Bicol Region is a hub for adventure, culture, and fiery food.
